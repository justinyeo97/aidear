# Third-party interaction components

Aidear incorporates the free Magnet and SpotlightCard components from [React Bits](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits), by David Haz, under the MIT + Commons Clause License Condition v1.0.

The full upstream notice is retained at `src/components/react-bits/LICENSE.md`. Source paths and the pinned upstream revision are recorded in `src/components/react-bits/SOURCE.md`. These components are part of the aidear application.

Research also included [Motion](https://motion.dev/), an MIT-licensed animation library. It was not added as a dependency because these React Bits components do not require it.
