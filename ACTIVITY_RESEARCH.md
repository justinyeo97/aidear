# Activity selection

The filters use concrete activities people can search for locally. They are an editorial selection from date guides, not a statistically ranked list of the most popular dates. Existing requested options such as archery and badminton are retained.

Sources reviewed:

- [The Knot: Date ideas](https://www.theknot.com/content/date-ideas): broad date inspiration, including mini golf, cooking and creative workshops.
- [Time Out Kuala Lumpur: Unusual date ideas](https://www.timeout.com/kuala-lumpur/things-to-do/unusual-date-ideas-in-kl): local inspiration, including pottery.
- [The Everygirl: Date ideas](https://theeverygirl.com/date-ideas/): bowling, mini golf, cooking classes and food outings among its suggestions.

Activity types: cafes, parks and picnics, museums and galleries, bookstores, archery, mini golf, bowling, badminton, roller skating, arcades, board games, dessert, pottery, dinner, movies, cooking classes and escape rooms.

Budget amounts are illustrative estimates for two, not researched venue prices. Availability, actual prices and suitability for two depend on the venue. Each activity has its own server-side search phrase; nearby results remain specific to the selected activity.
